Welcome!

I am a PhD candidate at Leiden Observatory as part of Dr. Melissa McClure's research group. My research interests lie in probing the chemical building blocks of planetesimals in protoplanetary disks. Specifically, I am creating a physical-chemical model to predict elemental abundances in the innermost disk regions in order to trace depletion expected in the presence of trapping mechanisms farther out in the disk. I hope to use models and observations in the bridging of astrochemistry and planet formation to contribute to the big question of how planets, such as Earth, formed in the first place. Check out my CV for more details!

I earned my M.Sc. in Astronomy Research from Leiden University in 2025. My M.Sc. thesis was supervised by Dr. Melissa McClure, and my first-year research project was supervised by Dr. Xander Tielens. Previously, I graduated as an I.I. Rabi Scholar in 2022 from Columbia University in the City of New York where I recieved my B.A. — Major in Astronomy, Concentration in Chemistry. I was raised in New York City and am a city girl at heart, but I also enjoy getting away from the urban scene for an adventurous hike every once and awhile.
